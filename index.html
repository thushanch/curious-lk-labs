Engineering Labs

Two interactive simulator suites for engineering students at the University of Moratuwa. Move a load, open a valve, rotate a stress state, and watch the theory answer back. Everything runs in the browser with no build step, no server and no tracking, so it all works offline once the page has loaded.

a project by Thushan Chamika

Each lab now has its own repository and its own site. CivilLab and Water Lab. This page is kept only as a way in to both.

The labs

Hydraulics & Hydrology

Water Lab

Forty one simulators running from a single pipe out to a whole modelled catchment. Pressure surges, pump curves, weirs, unit hydrographs and flood routing, each one solved live as you change the inputs.

  • Pipelines, EGL and HGL, thrust, venturi, siphons
  • Pumps, NPSH, impellers and water hammer
  • Open channels, weirs and hydraulic jumps
  • Rainfall, catchments, hydrographs and routing
41 simulators · one self-contained file Open Water Lab
Civil Engineering

CivilLab

Structural, geotechnical and design simulators built on the Civil Engineering Student Handbook. Every app is backed by an engine that is unit tested in Node against closed form textbook values before any drawing is done.

  • Beams, shear and moment diagrams, deflection
  • Mohr's circle, torsion, buckling, trusses
  • Moment distribution, plastic collapse, dynamics
  • Soil phase, particle size and shear strength
14 apps live · 20 more in the pipeline Open CivilLab
